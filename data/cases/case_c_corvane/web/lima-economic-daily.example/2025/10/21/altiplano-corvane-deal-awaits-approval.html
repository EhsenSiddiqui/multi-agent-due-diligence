Altiplano deal for Corvane unit still awaiting approval

Registry delay leaves ownership of the Huarani exploration company unchanged

By Diego Salas · 21 October 2025

Corvane Minerales, the exploration unit of Canada's Corvane Group, remains under Corvane ownership because the sale to Altiplano Resources is still waiting for a mining registry approval, according to two people familiar with the deal.

Altiplano gave a new timetable in an investor update this month. The unit is also named in a regulator's notice about the Huarani camp.
