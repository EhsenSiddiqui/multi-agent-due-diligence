Ministry of Labour fines Northstream Apparel after warehouse injury

By Sam Okafor · Thursday, March 14, 2019

Northstream Apparel Inc. was fined $45,000 after a warehouse worker was injured by an unguarded conveyor in 2018.

The Toronto clothing brand pleaded guilty to one charge under the Occupational Health and Safety Act, according to the Ontario Court of Justice bulletin.

The company said it has since fitted guards to every conveyor.
